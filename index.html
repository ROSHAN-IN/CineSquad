HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose a category and difficulty.
  2. Each round draws 20 unique movies at random from 120.
  3. Read the visual picture clue and pick one of four titles.
  4. Correct answer earns 100 points; using a hint lowers it to 70.
  5. A question times out automatically. Finish all 20 to see your score.

All illustrated clues work offline. You can replace them with your own permitted movie photos in assets/posters.
